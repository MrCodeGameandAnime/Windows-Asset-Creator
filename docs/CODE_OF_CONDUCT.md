# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

404 Builds is committed to making participation in the Windows Asset Creator project respectful and welcoming to everyone. We want people to be able to contribute, ask questions, report problems, and disagree constructively regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack or demean people.
- Give and receive feedback with an open mind.
- Respect different levels of experience and help keep project discussions focused and accessible.
- Follow the project’s contribution and security-reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, sexualized, or degrading language or imagery.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions, or persistently engaging in abusive conduct.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions when someone is representing or discussing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this code to the project maintainers privately through the repository’s GitHub contact channels. Please include links or relevant context and tell us if you need confidentiality. Do not use a public issue to disclose sensitive personal information.

The maintainers will review reports, respect the privacy of those involved as far as practical, and take action they consider appropriate. Actions may include a reminder, removal of content, a warning, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is not acceptable.
